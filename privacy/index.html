Privacy

Your scenario stays in the browser.

The form uses anonymous counts, rates and dates only. Cardflow does not upload or store those inputs. Aggregate page visits and calculator start/complete events may be counted without sending the values you enter. Closing or refreshing the page discards the current scenario.

Cardflow calculates from anonymous counts and rates in this browser. It does not scan, identify, grade, price or sell cards.
